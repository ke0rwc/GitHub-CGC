The Property

A cozy high-end cabin in the woods reimagined, this 1-bedroom, 1-bath vacation rental plays host to modern amenities and ultra-scenic views. Do your most productive work in the dedicated workspace with excellent internet. Perfect for professionals, house hunters, life transitions or vacationers. Make yourself comfortable practicing yoga on the provided mats, curl up with your lap top or book by the glow of the wood-burning fire. Recline on the deck with a satisfying beverage and enjoy the endless pines, or deer walking by. Post bedtime or after a hike, a bike ride or dinner enjoy the toasty heat of the private sauna. Large parking space for your trucks or SUV’s.

Newly Renovated in 2024 | Remote-Work & Family Friendly | Walk to local shops and library
